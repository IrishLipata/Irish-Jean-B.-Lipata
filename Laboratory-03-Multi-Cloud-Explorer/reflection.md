# Mission Reflection

Completing the Multi-Cloud Explorer mission helped me understand the differences and similarities between AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform. The cloud platform that impressed me the most was Google Cloud Platform because of its strong focus on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, data analytics, and Kubernetes. While researching the three platforms, I learned that each provider has different strengths and services that can be useful depending on the situation.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three provide cloud services for computing, storage, databases, networking, identity management, and application development. They also provide global infrastructure that allows organizations to run their applications and services in different locations. I noticed that although their services have different names, they often provide similar functions.

I learned that business requirements are very important when selecting a cloud platform. A company should not simply choose a provider because it is popular. For example, I recommended Azure for the university because it already uses Microsoft technologies, while I recommended GCP for the AI research company because of its AI and Machine Learning capabilities. This showed me that the best cloud platform depends on the needs, budget, existing technology, and goals of the organization.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me how to analyze a client's situation before making a recommendation. I learned to compare services and think about scalability, compatibility, performance, and business needs instead of just looking at the names of the cloud providers.

After completing this mission, I think my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has improved because it now contains more organized research, comparisons, recommendations, and documentation. I also became more comfortable using Markdown and organizing my work in GitHub. Overall, this mission helped me understand how cloud technologies can be applied to real business situations.
